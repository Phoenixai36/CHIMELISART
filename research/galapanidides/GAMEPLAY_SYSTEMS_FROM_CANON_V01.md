# GALAPANÍDIDES — Gameplay Systems Derived From Canon v0.1

## Principle

Mechanics are derived from explicit world rules found in the photographed book. Where a mechanic goes beyond the text, it is labelled **ADAPTATION**.

## Core systems

### 1. Morphic embodiment — CANON-DERIVED

The civilization can decompose/recompose cells and assume desired forms.

Game system:
- temporary embodiment states;
- different silhouettes and traversal profiles;
- form-specific interaction affordances;
- visibility changes;
- size changes for scout forms.

### 2. Mind fusion — CANON-DERIVED

The escaping group fuses its minds into a single entity.

Game system:
- multiple consciousness states represented as one controllable composite;
- internal voices/perspectives can affect decisions;
- temporary separation/recombination can become a later mechanic.

### 3. Portal navigation — CANON-DERIVED

Portals connect universes/realities and can be damaged or destroyed.

Game system:
- portal graph rather than conventional level-select map;
- portal stability as a world-state variable;
- damaged portals produce incomplete/unstable transitions;
- different realities expose different spatial rules.

### 4. Reality shifting — CANON-DERIVED

The civilization can cross universes and parallel worlds.

Game system:
- the same location can have multiple reality states;
- geometry, entities and environmental rules may differ between states;
- player navigation can reveal correspondence between realities.

### 5. Temporal embodiment — CANON-DERIVED

Galapanídides can move their cellular existence to past, present or future before recomposition.

Game system:
- temporal echoes;
- environment snapshots;
- state-dependent routes;
- optional temporal reconstruction puzzles.

### 6. Infiltration / identity uncertainty — CANON-DERIVED

Matèria Fosca warriors can adopt human appearance and infiltrate powerful institutions.

Game system:
- appearance is not a reliable identity signal;
- evidence must come from behavior, dimensional signatures, relationships or other observable inconsistencies;
- avoid simplistic visual "good/bad" markers.

### 7. Rastrejadors — CANON-DERIVED

Galapanídides create scouts that vary form and size and enter Earth's atmosphere to observe changes in both worlds.

Game system:
- reconnaissance mode;
- scale-changing traversal;
- atmospheric observation points;
- information gathering before direct intervention.

## World-state systems

### Portal network

Represent worlds as nodes and viable portals as edges:

```text
Reality A ── portal ── Reality B
   │                     │
 damaged               stable
   │                     │
 Reality C ── portal ── Reality D
```

The destruction of a portal changes reachable space rather than merely closing a door.

### Hidden conflict

Humanity's ordinary layer should remain functional while the dimensional conflict progresses underneath it.

This supports:
- public world / hidden world duality;
- observation before intervention;
- environmental storytelling;
- infiltration consequences without constant combat.

## ADAPTATION systems to consider later

These are not stated as mechanics by the book and therefore remain optional:

- stealth based on dimensional signatures;
- investigation system;
- social deduction around infiltrators;
- mind-fusion skill tree;
- portal repair/construction;
- reality-state puzzles;
- non-linear timeline traversal;
- faction reputation.

## Explicitly rejected for now

- conventional XP grind;
- generic loot rarity;
- arbitrary crafting;
- generic fantasy classes;
- enemy health bars as the primary expression of conflict;
- combat-first design.

The source material points more naturally toward exploration, identity uncertainty, dimensional traversal, transformation and strategic discovery.
