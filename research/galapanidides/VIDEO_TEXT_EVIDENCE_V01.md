# GALAPANÍDIDES — Video Text Evidence v0.1

This register records passages that were visually legible during the second scan of the five user-provided videos. Timestamps below refer to extracted 1-frame-per-second samples and are corpus coordinates, not printed page numbers.

## Evidence A — civilization

Source: `01.46.02`, frame `0025`.

Legible content establishes:
- a civilization at a remote/infinite location in the universe;
- extremely advanced intelligence and knowledge;
- cellular decomposition/recomposition;
- practical invisibility;
- movement of cellular existence to past, present or future;
- arbitrary recomposition of form;
- gigantic spacecraft;
- traversal of universes and parallel worlds;
- their technology/mode of existence being envied by other universes.

## Evidence B — planet and enemy

Source: `01.46.02`, frame `0027`/`0028`.

Legible content establishes:
- the place is called planet Galapanídides;
- inhabitants are practically immortal;
- immortality depends on the organism not being destructively attacked;
- they lived eternally and harmoniously;
- destructive beings appear and annihilate/raze what is in their path;
- those beings are called Matèria Fosca;
- most inhabitants die horribly;
- survivors devise a plan to leave the planet and begin again elsewhere.

## Evidence C — exodus

- Matèria Fosca warriors pursue them;
- Galapanídides abilities/technology allow passage through portals to universes with different realities.

## Evidence F — Galapanídides trackers and UFO narrative

Source: `01.50.31`, frame `0023`.

Legible content establishes:
- Galapanídides create groups of trackers (`rastrejadors`) that change form and size;
- they enter Earth's atmosphere and examine changes in both worlds;
- the story links this activity to unidentified objects appearing on Earth at different times;
- it references U.S. Department of Defense/Pentagon reports and counts of unidentified phenomena.

These real-world references are narrative content of the book, not independently verified evidence for the story's explanation.

## Evidence G — Matèria Fosca access and COVID-19 narrative

Source: `01.50.31`, frame `0039`.

Legible content establishes the novel's fictional causal narrative that:
- Matèria Fosca finds a portal connecting to the human world;
- its physical appearance is invisible to human eyes;
- the first portal they manage to cross is placed by the text in China;
- the narrative then associates the crossing with COVID-19;
- the text gives a death count by May 2022.

This is explicitly recorded as **fictional narrative content**. It must never be presented by the game or Atlas as a verified medical/historical explanation of COVID-19.

## Evidence H — later viral strategy

Source: `01.50.31`, frame `0058`.

The photographed paragraph states that Matèria Fosca warriors/virus carriers continued attempting to infect humanity after suffering losses, then changed strategy and produced different versions of the same virus whose effects differed.

Because some words are obscured by lighting/blur, this entry preserves only the reliable semantic content and does not claim a word-perfect transcription.

## Evidence J — Tossa de Mar anchor

Source: `01.47.51`, frame `0063`.

The visible passage identifies a Galapanídides spacecraft as buried near a small place identified as Tossa de Mar on the Costa Brava in Catalonia, associated with a large medieval wall. It continues by connecting the same coordinates to an imperceptible route/entry relevant to the Matèria Fosca.

Because the lower/right portion of the frame is curved and partially obscured, the exact final wording must be re-photographed before being promoted to a precise geographical claim.
