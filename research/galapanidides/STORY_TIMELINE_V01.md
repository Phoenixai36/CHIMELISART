# GALAPANÍDIDES — Story Timeline v0.1

## Phase 0 — Galapanídides before the catastrophe

A highly advanced civilization exists on planet Galapanídides. Its inhabitants are practically immortal, can manipulate/recompose their cellular existence, and possess technology for inter-universe and temporal traversal.

## Phase 1 — Arrival of Matèria Fosca

Destructive beings called Matèria Fosca attack the idyllic planet and annihilate much of its population.

## Phase 2 — Exodus

A surviving group decides to escape. Their minds are fused into a single entity. They travel for years through unknown universes using sophisticated spacecraft and portals.

## Phase 3 — Dispersal / arrival in different realities

The Galapanídides use portals to reach universes with different realities. Their existence becomes distributed across a broader dimensional network.

## Phase 4 — Earth observation

Galapanídides establish activity in the human world. They create trackers (`rastrejadors`) able to alter form and size, entering Earth's atmosphere to observe changes in both worlds.

## Phase 5 — Hidden coexistence

Humans continue ordinary life without understanding the surrounding dimensional conflict. Galapanídides remain alert because inter-world travel has become difficult.

## Phase 6 — Portal collapse

Matèria Fosca damages/destroys portals. Only limited infiltration routes remain. This creates an increasingly constrained dimensional topology.

## Phase 7 — Human-form infiltration

Some Matèria Fosca warriors adopt human appearance and infiltrate high-level human institutions. Appearance can no longer be trusted as a reliable identity test.

## Phase 8 — Matèria Fosca reaches humanity

The narrative describes Matèria Fosca finding a portal into the human world and connects that event, fictionally, to the COVID-19 pandemic. This remains a story element, not a factual claim.

## Phase 9 — Viral adaptation

The narrative describes continued infection attempts, losses among the attackers, and subsequent variants/versions of the virus with different effects.

## Phase 10 — Space-time disruption

The future becomes uncertain/invisible; Matèria Fosca neutralizes some forms of space-time. Galapanídides become trapped in another universe while Matèria Fosca retains access to humanity.

## Phase 11 — Tossa de Mar node

The narrative identifies a buried Galapanídides spacecraft near Tossa de Mar on the Costa Brava and associates the coordinates with an imperceptible route relevant to the dimensional conflict.

## Game chronology principle

The adaptation should not force these phases into a simple linear campaign. The source rules naturally support:

- revealed chronology;
- flashbacks through temporal displacement;
- parallel reality versions of the same event;
- investigation of hidden Earth-side consequences;
- portal-dependent route changes.
